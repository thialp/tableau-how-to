# How-To #1: multi-select filters from shapes

Build a filter panel in Tableau from worksheets, four images and a few parameters: a button that opens a panel, lists you tick and untick, departments that follow their function, a last option that can't be unticked, and options that gray out when nobody is behind them.

**[▶ Try it live on Tableau Public](https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/thialp/viz/HowtoBuildMulti-SelectFiltersfromShapesArcadiaHow-To/Multi-selectfilters)**

## ⬇️ Download

**[Download everything (multi-select-filters-files.zip)](https://github.com/thialp/tableau-how-to/raw/main/01-multi-select-filters/multi-select-filters-files.zip)**, under 1 MB: the data (`arcadia_employees.csv`) and the images (folder `Arcadia How-To`).

## Get started

1. Unzip the file.
2. Move the folder **Arcadia How-To** into `Documents/My Tableau Repository/Shapes/` (on Windows, `Documents\My Tableau Repository\Shapes\`), then restart Tableau.
3. Open a new Tableau workbook, connect to `arcadia_employees.csv`, and follow the steps.

## The steps

- **In Tableau:** [download the workbook from Tableau Public](https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/thialp/viz/HowtoBuildMulti-SelectFiltersfromShapesArcadiaHow-To/Multi-selectfilters) (the download icon under the viz) and open its tabs **Step 1** to **Step 17**. Each sheet's caption, on its left, has that step's instructions and every formula to paste.
- **On this page:** the same 17 steps, with the reasons behind them, are in the **[step-by-step guide](GUIDE.md)**.

## Use it on your own data

The data is a plain list, one row per person, like most data you will meet. Swap function and department for any parent and child in yours, and region, country and office for any three levels. Only the field names change.
